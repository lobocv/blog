# Capture an idea

Append the idea to `writing/ideas.md`. This should take seconds, not a conversation.

- With an argument: add one bullet at the top of the `## Ideas` list, as
  `- YYYY-MM-DD: <idea>`. Use Calvin's words exactly. Don't expand, polish,
  title or scope it. Fix only obvious typos
- If it clearly belongs to a series listed in the file, add the series tag
  (eg `[trellis]`). Don't invent new series
- Reply with one line confirming it was added. No suggestions, no follow-up
  questions, no "this could also cover..."
- With no argument: print the list and stop. Choosing between ideas is `/scope`'s job
