I'm a software engineer and architect. What I care about most is the shape of a
system: how the pieces fit together, and whether it will still make sense a few
years from now.

Most architecture problems I've run into turned out to be communication problems.
So I put as much effort into how a team works together (eg design reviews, writing
decisions down, keeping process light enough that people actually follow it) as I
do into the code. I also like the product side of engineering, figuring out what's
worth building before deciding how to build it.

Outside of work I'm a dad to three boys and a serial hobbyist with too little time.
These days that means wood carving, electric guitar and whichever part of the house
I'm renovating. When I can, I'm outdoors.
